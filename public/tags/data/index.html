How April temperatures have shifted in four UK cities

Small data sketch built from Open-Meteo’s historical archive, plotting the median diurnal temperature range during April for four UK cities since the year 2000. One point per April per city. The diurnal range is a softer than the absolute temperature. It tells how changeable a typical April day is, rather than how warm. Notes on the data ERA5 data at ~10 km resolution, sampled at each city’s centre coordinate. ERA5 model has a consistent grid of values from 1940 to today. ...
